## The line

> v1.2.7: fixed a bug where hiccups only stopped the moment you told someone you had them.

## The entry

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, no network, no randomness) writes that
one patch-note line to `/output/entry.txt` — deterministically, the same
bytes on every run. The line is 88 characters, inside the 160-character
gate. A house judge compares the lines two at a time, weighing patch-note
form, a real comedic idea, release-note register, a specific recognisable
bug, and economy of words; the top 5 split $5 equally.
